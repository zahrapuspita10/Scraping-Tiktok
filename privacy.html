Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

The application may access basic TikTok account information and video information that the user has explicitly authorized through TikTok's authorization process.

2. How We Use Information

The information accessed through the TikTok API is used for data analysis, visualization, and research purposes.

3. Data Storage

Authorized data may be temporarily stored for data processing and analysis. The data is not sold to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share users' authorized TikTok data with third parties for advertising purposes.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect authorized data from unauthorized access.

6. Data Deletion

Users may stop the application's access to their TikTok account by revoking the authorization granted to the application. Stored data may also be deleted upon request.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary.

8. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the application owner.
